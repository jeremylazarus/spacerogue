# World bible (SPOILERS)

## Premise
The **Shoal** is a tangled, shallow region of space where hyperspace "lanes" knot and drift.
Lanes only run one way, deeper toward the **Heart** (each run is a voyage from the Shoal's edge to its Heart: depth 0..7).

The player is a **Wayfarer**: a captain who wakes in a cradle-pod at the edge of the Shoal with only
fragments of memory. When the Wayfarer dies, they wake again — a new body, a new ship, a slightly
different galaxy (the Shoal reshuffles: new factions, new names). What carries over are **Echoes**
(memory fragments, the meta currency) and the **Codex** (lore remembered across lives).

Who re-instantiates the Wayfarer? The **Choir** — a vast chorus heard in hyperspace static. Locals
know it as a superstition ("the singing in the lanes"). Zealots worship it. Machines study it.
Hives seem to hear it more clearly than anyone.

**Wardens** are silent, faceless enforcer ships that appear when a traveller lingers too long in one
system. Nobody knows who sends them. Lore answer (revealed slowly): they are the Choir's immune system;
lingering = "becoming a stable note", which the Choir corrects.

**The Tide** is the slow exhalation of the Shoal: systems behind you fade/collapse. Lanes close behind.

## The cross-run mystery (meta)
The **First Voyage**: long ago, the first Wayfarer reached the Heart and *bound* the Choir — or merged
with it. Seven codex fragments `frag.origin.1`..`frag.origin.7` reveal this across many runs (found
rarely, in special events, some only after certain meta flags). With >= 5 fragments, the **origin**
main arc becomes eligible. Its finale: the Choir Avatar. Truth: the Choir *is* every Wayfarer who
ever died in the Shoal, including all of the player's past lives; the cycle is the first Wayfarer's
attempt to find a captain who reaches the Heart *and chooses differently*. Endings: free the Choir
(the Shoal unknots; cycle ends), take its place (become the next binder), or break it (destroy; unknown).

Recurring cross-run touches (use metaFlags): NPCs who say "you again?", a ghost ship bearing your
previous ship's name, a station keeper who keeps a ledger of your deaths.

## Tone
Melancholy wonder with dry humor. Star Control II–style aliens: each faction has a distinct voice.
Short punchy paragraphs (2-5 sentences). Second person ("you"). Avoid exposition dumps.

## Faction voices
- mercantile: polite, transactional, idioms about ledgers and margins.
- zealot: archaic, ecstatic, talks of "the Song", "the Faithless", "the Heart".
- hive: plural "we", sensory words (taste, warmth), unsettling sincerity, grammar slightly off.
- machine: precise, numbered statements, curiosity about irrational behavior, probabilities.
- raider: swagger, slang, honor codes, tribute, toasts.
- ancient: few words, cryptic, slow, tests and riddles, refers to ages.
